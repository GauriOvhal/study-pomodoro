**Focus Pomodoro**

A Pomodoro timer designed for deep work. 
This app features a supersized display, custom goal setting, and a smooth, second-by-second progress bar.

**Key Features**
- **Dynamic Goal Setting**: Choose your study duration (from 30 mins to several hours) and the app automatically calculates your 25/5 minute cycles.
- **Supersized UI**: Large, bold typography makes the timer easy to read from across the room.
- **Smooth Progress Bar**: A horizontal yellow bar that fills simultaneously with every passing second.
- **Dark Mode Support**: Toggle between Light and Dark modes for comfortable viewing at any time.
- **Customizable Alerts**: Choose between multiple alarm sounds or use Mute mode for silent focus.

**Built With**
- HTML5 - Structure
- CSS3 - Custom variables, Dark Mode transitions, and animations
- JavaScript - Real-time countdown logic and state management


**How to Use**
1. Open the app and enter your study goal in hours.
2. Click Start Studying!
3. Use the Start/Pause button to control your 25-minute focus block.
4. The yellow progress bar will fill up as you work.
5. After each session, the app will alert you to take a 5-minute break.
6. Track your progress with the Sessions Remaining counter at the bottom.


**Project Structure**
- `index.html`: Main structure and screen transitions.
- `style.css`: All styling, including Dark Mode and the high-contrast progress bar.
- `script.js`: Logic for the timer, sound selection, and progress calculations.
